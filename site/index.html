Beyond Data Volume

Evaluating clustering robustness under missing-data mechanisms

Read the research report

What the simulations found

Across eight seeds, the study ran 480 comparisons of MCAR, MAR and MNAR masking and four imputation methods on synthetic convenience stores. Complete-data K-means served as the reference partition.

Imputation changes the partition

At 30% MCAR, mean adjusted Rand index (ARI) was 0.632 with zero fill and 0.861 with iterative imputation.

Silhouette can miss assignment changes

In one 40% MAR run with KNN imputation, silhouette rose from 0.160 to 0.167 while 13.5% of aligned store assignments changed.

More stores leave some distortion

Under 30% MNAR with zero fill, mean held-out ARI rose from 0.568 to 0.718 as training size increased from 500 to 18,000 stores.

Explore the results

The results apply to the simulated retail population and masking mechanisms. The complete-data partition is a comparison benchmark, and business decision thresholds are illustrative.
